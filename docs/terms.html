3. Privacy & Data

Red Grid Link is offline-first. No operational data (position, session, or configuration) is transmitted to or stored on any server controlled by us or any third party. Optional release-only crash diagnostics may transmit stack traces and device model info to Sentry; PII is disabled and GPS coordinates are stripped before transmission. See the Privacy Policy for the full disclosure.

Field Link peer-to-peer communication occurs directly between devices via Bluetooth Low Energy on every platform, with Apple Multipeer Connectivity (AWDL) on iOS and Google Play Services Nearby Connections on Android as a parallel transport. PIN and QR sessions wrap every payload in AES-256-GCM derived from an ECDH P-256 session key; Open mode is intentionally unencrypted for training and demos. Operational data persists locally as part of session history until you delete it; the encryption keys themselves are ephemeral and discarded on session end.

4. Field Link Limitations

The Field Link feature uses Bluetooth Low Energy on every platform with the platform-specific secondary transport described above. Actual range depends on terrain, vegetation, weather, and device hardware.

Field Link is not a substitute for proper communication equipment (radios, satellite communicators). Do not rely on Field Link for life-safety communications.
